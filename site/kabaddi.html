            How these numbers are built

            Where it comes from

            1,258Pro Kabaddi League matches
            12seasons, every raid and tackle

            Pro Kabaddi League Official Data — the scorecard and the raid-by-raid event stream of every match, joined on the league's own player ids. No name matching anywhere.

            What a player is measured on

            8metrics per role
            3 yrsbefore form counts half

            Not just points, but how they come — success rate, super raids, and the do-or-die raid a raider must convert or is out. Recent seasons count for more, and a player with a short record is treated cautiously rather than taken at face value.

            Compared within a role

            Raider258
            Cover157
            Corner115

            A raider and a cover defender play different games, so each role is a separate space. A player is only ever ranked against his own.

            Does it work

            4×better than guessing
            This tool23rd
            Guessing95th

            Take one raider, split his matches in half, and hide from the tool that the halves are the same player. Asked to rank everyone against the first half, it puts his own second half around 23rd of 190 — random would be 95th.
