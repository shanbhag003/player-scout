              Where it comes from

              22,905matches, every ball
              4.4Mdeliveries parsed

              Cricsheet for the cricket, Wikidata for ages and names, joined on an exact id. No name matching anywhere.

              What a player is measured on

              16things counted per batter
              3 yrsbefore form counts half

              Not just runs and wickets, but when in the match they came, and against which kind of bowling. Recent seasons count for more, and a player with a short record is treated cautiously rather than taken at face value.

              Does it work

              2.6×better than guessing
              This tool19th
              Guessing50th

              Take one player, split his career in half, and hide from the tool that the halves are the same person. Asked to rank everyone against the first half, it puts his own second half around 19th of 100 — random would be 50th.
